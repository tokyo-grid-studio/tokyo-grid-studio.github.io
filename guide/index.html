1. What you receive

This is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.

Single artwork

  • 1 JPG file
  • Download & Display Guide (PDF)

Set or bundle

  • 1 or more ZIP files with all the JPGs in the set
  • Download & Display Guide (PDF)

Every artwork is a 3840 × 2160 pixel JPG (4K, 16:9 landscape, sRGB). The exact file list is shown on each Etsy listing.

2. Download your files from Etsy

Use a web browser, not the Etsy app. Etsy does not let you download digital purchases in its app. Sign in at etsy.com in Safari, Chrome, or another browser on your phone or computer.

  1. Sign in to etsy.com and go to Your account → Purchases.
  2. Next to your order, select Download Files.
  3. On the Downloads page, download each file.

You can also use the View your files on Etsy button in the email Etsy sends when your files are ready. If you checked out as a guest, the download link is in your receipt email.

Where do the files go?

ComputerYour Downloads folder.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)Files app → Downloads folder. In Safari, you can also tap the Downloads button next to the search field.
Android (Chrome)Open the Files app. In Chrome, you can also tap More → Downloads.

For a set, downloading on a computer is usually easiest. Then copy the images to your phone or a USB drive.

3. Open ZIP files (sets and bundles)

Single artworks are plain JPG files and don't need this step.

WindowsRight-click the ZIP file → Extract All… → follow the steps.
MacDouble-click the ZIP file. The images appear in a folder next to it.
iPhone / iPadIn the Files app, tap the ZIP file. A folder with the images is created.
AndroidIn Files by Google, select the ZIP file → Extract → Done. The images are saved in the same folder.

4. Send the artwork to Samsung The Frame

Option A: SmartThings app (phone)

For The Frame 2018 and later. You need the SmartThings app, a Samsung account, and your TV added to the app.

  1. Save the JPG on your phone (unzip a set first).
  2. Open SmartThings and select your Frame TV.
  3. Tap Art Mode, then Add Your Photos.
  4. Select the artwork, then tap Save on The Frame.

If the image doesn't appear in the photo picker, save it to your phone's photo library first.

Option B: USB drive

  1. Copy the JPG files (unzipped) to a USB drive.
  2. Connect the drive to the TV (on models with a One Connect Box, connect it there).
  3. With The Frame in Art Mode, press Select on the remote.
  4. Go to My Collection and select the USB drive.
  5. Choose the images, select Save, then Save Selected. Don't remove the drive while saving.
  6. Find the saved images in My Collection → My Photos.

Menu names can differ by model, year, and region. If something looks different, check Samsung's support page for your model.

5. Recommended settings on The Frame

Matte (border)While the artwork is shown, press Up on the remote and select Matte (called Mount in some regions). Choose a style with Left/Right, then a color, and press Select. If your model offers an option with no matte, choose it to show the full image edge to edge.
Brightness and Color ToneIn SmartThings, select your Frame TV, tap Art Mode (top right) → Settings, then adjust Brightness and Color Tone. On some models, these are under Screen Settings on the TV.
Our suggestionStart at a medium brightness and a slightly warm color tone. That keeps the artwork soft and close to paper. Then adjust to your room.

In Art Mode, The Frame adjusts brightness and color tone to the room light by itself. Don't block the sensor on the front of the TV.

6. Other TVs and computers

Other art-mode TVs (Hisense CanvasTV, TCL NXTFRAME and others)

Many art-mode TVs can show your own images from a USB drive or the maker's app. The steps and menu names depend on the brand and model, so please follow your TV maker's instructions. Our files are standard 16:9 JPGs at 3840 × 2160, so they fit most 4K screens.

Regular TVs

Many TVs can open JPG files from a USB drive in their photo or media player. Choose a setting such as Fit to Screen, Just Scan, or Full Screen without zoom, so nothing is cropped.

Computers and monitors

Open the JPG full screen in your photo viewer, or set it as your desktop background. On a 16:9 screen, choose Fill or Fit. On other screen shapes, Fit shows the whole image.

7. Troubleshooting

The edges are cut off
  • On The Frame, try a different matte (or no matte, if your model offers that option).
  • On other TVs, turn off zoom and choose Fit to Screen, Just Scan or Full Screen.
  • Make sure you are showing the original 3840 × 2160 file, not a screenshot.
Colors look dull or too dark
  • Raise Brightness or change Color Tone in the Art Mode settings.
  • In a dark room, The Frame lowers the brightness by itself.
  • Colors can look slightly different on every screen.
The image looks blurry
  • Send the original JPG from your download. Screenshots and messaging apps can make the image smaller.
  • Don't edit or compress the file before sending it.
I can't download my files
  • Use a web browser, not the Etsy app. Sign in at etsy.com and open Purchases.
  • If the Download Files button is gray, your payment is still processing. Please try again later.
  • If nothing happens when you click a link, turn off ad blockers or other browser add-ons and try again.
  • Can't find the email? Check your spam or promotions folder. Your files are always in Purchases.
The ZIP file won't open
  • Make sure the download has finished, then try again with the steps in section 3.
  • On a phone, check that you have enough free space.
  • If it still fails, delete the file, download it again, or try on a computer.

8. FAQ

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. This is a digital download only.

Can I download my files again later?

Yes. Your files stay in Purchases on etsy.com, and you can usually download them again at any time. Please also keep a backup copy of the original files.

Does it work on a TV that isn't The Frame?

Yes, on most screens that can show JPG images. It looks best on a 16:9 4K screen. See section 6.

Can I download on my phone?

Yes, in your phone's web browser (not the Etsy app). For sets with ZIP files, a computer is often easier.

Can I use the artwork on more than one screen?

Yes. You may show it on more than one screen within your own home.

Can I print the artwork?

Yes, for your own personal enjoyment. Printing to sell is not allowed. The files are made for 4K TVs (3840 × 2160 pixels), so large prints may look less sharp.

Why did I get a ZIP file?

A set has several images, so they come packed in one or more ZIP files to keep the download simple. See section 3 to open them.
